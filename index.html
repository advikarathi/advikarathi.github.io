Our Mission

Empowering through

Founded in 2018 as a registered Section 8 non-profit, the DR Foundation uses chess as a vehicle for transformation. We believe a single strategic move — on or off the board — can change a young person's life. Our work reaches blind, disabled, and underprivileged youth across India, aligning with UN SDGs 3 and 4.

Flagship Program
♟

Project Checkmate

Launched April 2016, Project Checkmate uses chess to promote education and wellbeing for underprivileged and visually impaired youth. We've trained 40+ students, produced 2 FIDE-rated players, run the Queen's Gambit Girls Chess Club, coached the top 15 blind chess players in India, and partnered with Grandmasters for live sessions.

02
🎓

DRF Scholarships

Five editions (2018–2021) awarding cash prizes and year-long mentorship to differently-abled people aged 15–30 — from para-athletes to UPSC aspirants.

03
🫀

Health Initiatives

Mental health sessions, dental camps at NAB Delhi, menstrual hygiene education, and nutritional support — because a healthy mind powers a strong game.

Scholarship History

Five editions.
Hundreds of lives
touched.

Each scholarship includes a full year of mentorship, speaker events, and partner programme access — not just a cash prize. Selected by essay. Open to persons with disabilities aged 15–30.

  1. 2018
    Inaugural DRF Scholarships
    4 winners at NAB, RK Puram, New Delhi. Selected by essay on their lives and future goals.
    In-Person · NAB Delhi
  2. 2019
    Second DRF Scholarships
    Winners include Neeraj Sharma (chess) and Khushi Bisht. Year-long mentoring. Ayush Jaiswal later received a full scholarship to Ashoka University.
    In-Person · NAB Delhi
  3. 2020
    Third DRF Scholarships
    First fully virtual edition. Open to persons with disabilities across all of India. 4 cash winners + participation rewards for all.
    Virtual · Pan-India
  4. 2021
    Devajyoti Scholarships
    Co-organised with Wheeling Happiness Foundation (Dr. Deepa Malik, Paralympic Gold Medalist). 6 winners + 5 runners-up across sport, arts, UPSC, and entrepreneurship.
    Virtual · With Wheeling Happiness
  5. 2021
    Shaktinaari Scholarships
    Focused on women aged 18–30 with disabilities. "Shaktinaari" = Women Power. 3 winners including Shalini Shaw (aspiring PhD) and Ribalin Lyngdoh (MSW student).
    Virtual · Women-Focused
